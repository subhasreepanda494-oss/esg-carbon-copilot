Scoring & Methodology

ESG Score Limitations (Prototype)

The current ESG scoring system calculates two distinct scores per pillar: Data Quality and ESG Performance. Data Quality indicates the completeness and verifiability of uploaded data (calculated as the percentage of valid records out of total records submitted). ESG Performance measures the uploaded metrics relative to configured industry benchmarks (using a weighted average of normalized metric scores).

Data Quality Engine

The Risk Engine continuously monitors data ingestion. Data Quality scores decrease if there are missing values, unverified emission factors, invalid units, or other integrity errors. Records flagged as 'Review Required' or 'Unverified' must be corrected to achieve a 100% Data Quality score.

Environmental Calculations & Scoring

Environmental emissions are deterministically calculated based on Activity, Quantity, and Unit. The system maps these inputs to verified emission factors from the UK Government 2026 factors database. The total CO₂e is compared against an industry benchmark (e.g., lower emissions score higher) to compute the Environmental Performance score.

Social & Governance Metrics

Social and Governance data are ingested from uploaded CSVs. Each metric (e.g., employee turnover, board independence) contributes to the respective pillar's performance score. The score is calculated by normalizing each metric's value against its specific industry benchmark and applying a weight. For example, higher board independence increases the Governance score, while higher employee turnover decreases the Social score.
